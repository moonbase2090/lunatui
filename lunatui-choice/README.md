# lunatui-choice

Checkbox, Switch and Radio for lunatui. A [`Checkbox`] draws `[x]`, `[ ]` or `[-]` before its
label, a [`Switch`] draws a three-cell track with a knob at the on or off end, and a [`Radio`]
wraps the core `RadioGroup` on a surface of your choosing. Checked and on states use the primary
color, focus uses the ring color, and disabled choices dim. Clicks reuse the core `ButtonState`.
All colors come from `lunatui-ui-tokens`.
